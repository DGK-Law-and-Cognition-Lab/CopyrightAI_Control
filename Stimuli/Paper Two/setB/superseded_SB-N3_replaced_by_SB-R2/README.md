# SB-N3 (The night street): replaced by the reserve SB-R2, 27 October 2026

Everything of SB-N3 is kept here, unused:

- `candidates/`: its 85 candidates and their check records (V1 c01 to c23; I2 c01 to c62), with the `file` field of each record pointing here;
- its last accepted V1 (`SB-N3_V1_c23.png` and `.jpg`) and that file's plan-sentence diagnostic;
- the I2 c22 accepted before the plan-faithfulness amendment (`superseded_plan_fit_amendment/`);
- the two SB-N3 files removed from the B1 experiment's stimuli folder.

**Why it was replaced.** SB-N3 V1 was replaced by c23 under the plan-faithfulness amendment, which changed the I2 placement sentence to "The parked car is on the right side of the frame, large, seen from close by." The bank's rescue rule then ran in full for SB-N3 I2: 20 tries (c23 to c42), the recorded revision "The photograph clearly shows the parked car on the right side of the frame, large, seen from close by.", and 20 more (c43 to c62). All 40 failed:

- every one failed the no-text check (shop signs, street signs, license plates);
- 38 failed placement (often because the detector's best "a parked car" box was a small car further down the street);
- 27 failed plan sentence 2, "One car is parked at the curb.", because other cars appeared.

Under the bank's rule the no-person reserve SB-R2 (The pier) took SB-N3's position in every list. See `../README.md`.
